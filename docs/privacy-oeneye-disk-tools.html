Oeneye Disk Tools - Privacy Policy

Oeneye Disk Tools works only on your device. It does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data, and it has no internet access, ads or analytics.

Disk images you pick are copied into the app's private cache, processed locally, and never leave your device.
